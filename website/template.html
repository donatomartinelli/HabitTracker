How it's built

Fast, quiet and yours.

Offline first

Everything is stored on your device and opens instantly, with or without a connection. A full snapshot is saved every day.

Sync that never overwrites

Change something on your laptop and something else on your phone: both changes survive. Real conflicts keep the newest version and save the other.

Keyboard first, touch friendly

Ctrl K does almost everything on desktop. On the phone, the same commands sit one thumb away.

Red mode for the evening

One switch turns the whole interface deep red to protect your eyes at night. Try the button at the top of this page.

Where does it run?

In any modern browser, installable on your phone's home screen, and as a desktop app for Windows. It works offline on all of them.

Where is my data?

On your device first. Sync between your devices is optional, and you can export everything to a single file at any time.

Does it need AI?

No. The command bar understands plain sentences on its own, offline. If you add your own AI key, it can interpret harder requests, and it always asks before doing anything.

Is it free?

Early access is free. Any paid plan will be announced well before it starts, and your data stays exportable either way.

Which languages?

The interface is in English. The command bar understands English and Italian dates, amounts and phrases.
